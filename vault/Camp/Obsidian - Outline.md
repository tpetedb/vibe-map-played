# Obsidian - Outline

Outline is a core plugin that lists the headings of the active note; click a heading to jump to that section or drag headings to rearrange sections.

## Facts

- Lists the headings in the active note
- Click a heading in the outline to navigate to that section
- Click and drag a heading within the outline to rearrange sections in the note

## Try in five minutes

Open a note with at least three headings, open the Outline tab, click a heading to jump to it, then drag the last heading above the first and check that the note's sections moved.
