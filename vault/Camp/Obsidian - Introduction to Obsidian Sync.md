# Obsidian - Introduction to Obsidian Sync

Obsidian Sync is a paid add-on service that privately syncs a vault across devices, with selective sync, version history, shared vaults, regional servers and a headless mode.

## Facts

- An add-on service at obsidian.md/sync that privately syncs notes across devices
- Guides: Set up Obsidian Sync, Switch to Obsidian Sync, Selective sync, Plans and storage limits, Version history, Status icon and messages, Security and privacy, Collaboration, Sync regions, Headless Sync, Troubleshoot Obsidian Sync
- Do not combine it with Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive on the same vault; back up first and fully migrate
- Unavailable on Apple devices with Lockdown mode unless Obsidian is exempted
- Server status at status.obsidian.md

## Try in five minutes

Read the Set up Obsidian Sync page linked from the intro, then check whether another sync tool (Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive) already watches your vault folder and make a backup before enabling Sync.
