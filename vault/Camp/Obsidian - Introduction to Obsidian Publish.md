# Obsidian - Introduction to Obsidian Publish

Obsidian Publish is a cloud hosting service that turns selected notes into a public wiki, knowledge base, documentation site or digital garden, hosted at publish.obsidian.md/your-site.

## Facts

- Select the notes to share, press Publish, and the site is hosted at publish.obsidian.md/your-site
- help.obsidian.md is itself an Obsidian Publish site; its vault is on GitHub (obsidianmd/obsidian-docs)
- Guides: Set up Obsidian Publish, Manage sites, Collaborate on a Publish site, Customize your site, Custom domains, Permalinks, Analytics, SEO, Security and privacy

## Try in five minutes

Open the Publish intro and follow the Set up Obsidian Publish link, then pick three notes you would select to share and check each for links to notes you would not publish.
