# Obsidian - Quick switcher

Quick switcher is a core plugin for finding and opening notes by name or alias from the keyboard, and for creating a note when nothing matches.

## Facts

- Open with Ctrl+O (Cmd+O on macOS) or the Open Quick switcher ribbon icon; on mobile tap the plus icon at the bottom
- Type a name or alias, move with the arrow keys, press Enter to open
- Enter with no match creates a note; Shift+Enter creates a note with the exact name even if similar notes exist
- Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on macOS) opens the selected note in a new tab
- An empty search term lists the most recent notes; press Down then Enter to toggle between two notes
- Autocomplete switches to a simpler algorithm once the vault reaches 10,000 items

## Try in five minutes

Press Ctrl/Cmd+O, type part of a note name and open it with Enter; press Ctrl/Cmd+O again, press Down and Enter to jump back; then type a new name and press Shift+Enter to create that note.
