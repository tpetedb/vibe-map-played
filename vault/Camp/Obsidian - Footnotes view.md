# Obsidian - Footnotes view

Footnotes view is a core plugin that lists all footnotes in the active note, lets you edit a footnote's text and jump to its position in the note.

## Facts

- Lists all footnotes in the active note
- Click a footnote to edit its text
- You can navigate to the footnote's position in the note

## Try in five minutes

Add two footnotes to a note, open the Footnotes view tab, click one footnote to edit its text, then use it to jump to where that footnote sits in the note.
